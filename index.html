Who Is DnB Santa?

Every December, Santa loads up on protein and carbs to bulk up for one impossible night — then spends Christmas Eve burning every last bit of it delivering presents around the world. It takes so much out of him that the whole rest of the year is recovery: eleven months travelling the world, watching out for kindness, teaching a bit of goodness where he can — and yes, clubbing, raving, and popping up wherever the party is. Come festival season he's back to his normal weight, ready to do it all again.

He's not the best DJ. He's not the best MC. He's the only DnB Santa.

🎭 The honest bit

Father Christmas has been remade plenty of times already — a real bishop, tangled up with older midwinter folklore, then redrawn by a soft-drink advert into the suit everyone recognises now. There was never one true version to copy. So here's the honest one: DnB Santa is Chris P Tee — also Doc Strange, also just Chris. Same person, different room.

When it's free, it's whoever feels right that day — not a script, not a performance for anyone. When it's booked and paid for as an act, you get DnB Santa properly, played his way. Either way, nobody's being tricked. That's rather the point.
